GridGeometryUtils.GridGeometryUtils — Module
GridGeometryUtils

Geometric primitives and predicates for working with shapes on rectangular grids: point containment (inside), measures (area, perimeter, volume), and segment/rectangle intersection (intersection, boundary_param, intersecting_area).

Comparisons throughout are made up to a relative tolerance of about 1000 * eps, so points within round-off of a boundary are treated as lying on it.
